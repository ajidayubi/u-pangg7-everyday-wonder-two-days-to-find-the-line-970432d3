Entrants write a single sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new: a lift door opening on a stranger's song, the pause before a kettle clicks, a puddle holding the whole sky. That sentence is the entry and sits in the entrant's repository. An AI judge compares entries two at a time against a brief published with the bounty, weighing whether the angle is one the reader has not met before, whether the surprise lands in a single read, and whether it stays grounded in something real. The sentence that wins the most comparisons takes the top reward, and the pool is split over the top places. Entry is free.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
